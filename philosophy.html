The 4 P’s

Preparation · Practice · Patience · Positivity. How I coach, invest, train, and show up online. The point is stacking them, not posting them.

01 · Preparation

Program before load

Unpaid work that makes paid work cheap — write the block, decide why before how much, clear the checklist before you hit publish.

02 · Practice

Show up for the boring sets

Repetition of the process when nobody is watching. Consistency compounds; last-minute scrambles teach the wrong lesson.

03 · Patience

Trust the block

Refuse to abandon a sound plan because the middle is ugly. Hold when the plan still makes sense — wait for the real green light.

04 · Positivity

Coach the next rep

Not cheerleading — standards high, tone forward. After a miss, name it once, then the next controllable step.
